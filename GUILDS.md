# Guild Questlines (about 12 quests each)

All guilds run parallel to the main story. All numbers (rewards, levels) follow Skyrim values. No magic for the player in any guild. Guild names and arcs are NEW unless noted.

---
## 1. The Sword-singers' Circle (Act One opens, canon basis)
**Theme:** discipline against rage. **Leader:** Khadija is the exile. The Circle's current head is **Elder Samira Rhun** (P), who exiled Khadija for a duel that killed a student. **Rank rewards:** Blades of the Circle (Redguard curved swords at Skyrim steel, Orcish and Ebony values), Block and One-handed perk training, unique armour (a light armour set at Elven values).
1. **The Open Gate** (level 4). A test: spar three students without killing anyone.
2. **Breath and Salt.** Learn the Circle's creed: a blade is a promise.
3. **The Student Who Fell.** Find the exiled student Khadija killed. He lives, a crippled hermit.
4. **Ghost Duels.** Win four duels at four shrines against ancient Dead.
5. **Shamed Banner.** Recover the Circle's banner from a bandit chief.
6. **A Lesson in Mercy.** Defeat a raider without killing him and take him to be judged.
7. **Whispers in the Hall.** A rival master is selling Circle secrets to Kerrash's men.
8. **The Duel at Dawn.** Duel the traitor.
9. **Khadija's Return.** Argue for Khadija's reinstatement.
10. **The Hundred Strokes.** A trial of endurance (Stamina and Block checks).
11. **The Corrupted Champion.** Kerrash's champion, a former Circle student.
12. **Finale: Yield or Kill** (Act Three duel at Hegathe's gate). **Yield** wins the Circle's blessing and a Judged-ending point. **Kill** keeps the player's rage and a Salt-ending point.

## 2. The Wreckers (Act Two opens)
**Theme:** loyalty and profit. **Leader:** Juma Redtide. **Base:** Redtide Anchorage. **Rewards:** ship passage, fences, a hideout, Sneak, Lockpicking and Speech training, light armour.
1. **Free Passage** (A2.1).
2. **Wet Cargo.** Recover a sunk shipment.
3. **Salt Lies.** Mark Kerrash's barrels along the coast.
4. **The Tide Tax.** Break a rival crew's extortion.
5. **Whose Anchor?** A theft inside the crew.
6. **The Dry Run.** Smuggle three people through a Sentinel checkpoint.
7. **Redtide Rising.** A storm run with a ship to defend.
8. **Teeth.** Rescue a crew member from the Sentinel jail.
9. **The Tally.** Audit the crew's books and find the skim.
10. **Salt War.** Hit Kerrash's salt barges.
11. **A Good Day for Fire.** Burn the stockpile at Hegathe's dock.
12. **Finale: Sell or Burn.** Sell Kerrash's salt barges for a fortune, or burn them. Burn dries up Kerrash's money in Act Three and gives a Ledger-ending point.

## 3. The Quiet Hand (Act Two opens, dark)
**Theme:** the price of a life. This guild goes as dark as the game allows. **Leader:** **the Barber of Sentinel** (P), a polite, funny, terrifying man who runs the Hand from a shaving shop. Members take **Contracts**. Every contract comes with a **reason the target deserves it**, and the player slowly learns that some of those reasons are lies. **Rewards:** Sneak, Light Armor and Pickpocket training, poisons, a hooded light armour set, the Dagger of the Hand (Daedric dagger values: weight 6, damage 11), the best sneak-attack perk training.

**The twist.** Kerrash is a long-time client of the Hand. The player's wife was almost one of its contracts twenty years ago, and the Hand declined it. The Barber knew her.

1. **A Good Shave.** The Barber tests the player with a first, easy, deserved kill.
2. **The Customer is Always.** A loan-shark in Sentinel. Clean, quiet.
3. **Old Blood.** A Forebear heir who beat his servants. Deserved.
4. **Fine Print.** A merchant who looks guilty. Evidence is thin. The Barber says "trust me".
5. **The Widow's Price.** A contract on a widow. The player investigates. She is innocent, and the contract is a business rival's.
6. **Cost of Doing.** The player may **refuse** (the Barber is disappointed and charming), **fulfil** it, or **take the rival instead**.
7. **Crimson Receipts.** The Hand's ledger. The player learns the Hand has killed for Kerrash.
8. **Three for the Table.** Three contracts at a wedding. Collateral risk is high.
9. **The Witness.** A contract on a grown man whose only crime is having been a witness at Hegathe. He is one of the last survivors, and Kerrash is paying. Dark choice.
10. **Silence Fee.** A Quiet Hand veteran asks the player to take his place, because he cannot do it any more.
11. **Bad Medicine.** The Barber orders the player to poison a water well. **This is the darkest quest.** Dozens of innocents. The player may comply (Collateral surges, huge reward, Infamy), refuse, or turn on the Barber.
12. **Finale: The Last Customer.** The Barber offers the player a contract on **one of his closest allies** (Zeyna, Leyla or Khadija, whichever the player trusts most) as a loyalty test. Refuse and fight the Hand. Accept and the player carries a permanent mark (a betrayal that shows in the ending and in Act Three).

**How dark.** Collateral rises fast here, and the guild is the main source of Collateral for the "Salt" ending. Harm to children is never shown or implied as a victim. Killings of non-combatants are shown only as aftermath or cut away, with a clear consequence line afterwards.

## 4. The Alik'r (Act Two opens, canon basis)
**Theme:** water and law. **Leader:** Mother Anwar. **Base:** Seven Wells and travelling camps. **Rewards:** desert travel (a free camel, a Yokudan Charger at high Standing), Archery, Block and Alchemy training, Alik'r armour at light Elven values.
1. **Water First.** Fix a collapsed well.
2. **Caravan Guard.** Guard a caravan through the dunes against raiders.
3. **The Lost Child.** Find a lost child. (She is found. This quest sets the tone of the Alik'r: no one is thrown away.)
4. **Dune Hunt.** Kill a Duneripper at a trade route.
5. **Salt in the Sand.** A well is poisoned. Find the saboteur.
6. **The Ash'abah.** Reconcile with the outcast nomads.
7. **A Debt of Water.** Settle a feud.
8. **The Yokudan Charger.** Break and earn a horse.
9. **The Thirst Trial.** Stand beside someone judged by desert law.
10. **The Singing Stones.** A ritual at an old shrine to Tu'whacca's Throne (lore).
11. **Sulayman's Table** (main quest A2.3).
12. **Finale: The Judgement.** Mother Anwar asks the player to judge a thief by desert law. This is the **Desert Law** ending's trial in miniature. The way the player rules decides whether that ending is open.
