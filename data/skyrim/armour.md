## Smithing unlocks [V]
Steel (perk only), Dwarven and Elven at Smithing 30, Orcish at 50, Glass at 70, Ebony at 80, Daedric at 90.

## Armour and perks
- Heavy Armor tree: Juggernaut (5 ranks), Fists of Steel, Well Fitted, Tower of Strength, Conditioning, Matching Set, Reflect Blows, Cushioned. Light Armor tree: Agile Defender (5), Custom Fit, Matching Set, Wind Walker, Deft Movement, Unhindered. Effects [X].
- Skill 100 in both armour skills removes armour weight while worn, and in Skyrim gives light armour a bonus of about 50 percent effectiveness [M, see UESP]. Treat as [X] until extracted.

## Hammerfell flavour (NEW, uses existing stat blocks)
Redguard and Alik'r gear is a **reskin** of a Skyrim set, so the numbers stay Skyrim's: Alik'r Warrior armour uses the light tier it corresponds to (Skyrim has Alik'r Warrior NPCs [M]). Scimitar uses the Scimitar stat line in `weapons.md`. Desert veils, sashes and lamellar are cosmetic variants on the Leather, Elven and Orcish rows. New named pieces must be given a donor row so balance is already known.
