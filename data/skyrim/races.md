Starting Health 100 and Stamina 100 for all races [M]. Skills start at 15 plus the bonus below.

## Adaptation for this game (magic skills removed)
Bonuses in removed skills are replaced, point for point, by thematically close non-magic skills. Races with no magic bonuses are unchanged.

| Race | Bonuses in this game |
|---|---|
| Altmer | Speech +10, Alchemy +5, Smithing +5 |
| Argonian | Lockpicking +10, Alchemy +5, Light Armor +5, Pickpocket +5, Sneak +5 |
| Bosmer | Archery +10, Alchemy +5, Light Armor +5, Lockpicking +5, Pickpocket +5, Sneak +5 (unchanged) |
| Breton | Alchemy +10, Speech +10 |
| Dunmer | One-handed +10, Alchemy +5, Light Armor +5, Sneak +5 |
| Imperial | Speech +10, Block +5, Heavy Armor +5, One-handed +5, Smithing +5 |

| Redguard | One-handed +15, Archery +5, Block +5, Light Armor +5, Smithing +5 |

## Racial abilities
Kept from Skyrim [V] unless stated.
| Race | Passive | Power (once a day in Skyrim) |
|---|---|---|
| Altmer | Replace magicka bonus with Stamina regeneration +10 percent [NEW] | Highborn: replaced by Stamina Surge (restore 50 Stamina) [NEW] |
| Argonian | Resist Disease 50 percent, Waterbreathing | Histskin: restore Health faster for 60 seconds |
| Bosmer | Resist Disease and Poison 50 percent | Command Animal: an animal fights for you for 60 seconds |
| Breton | Resist Magic 25 percent | Dragonskin: absorb spell damage for 60 seconds, useful because NPCs use magic |
| Dunmer | Resist Fire 50 percent | Ancestor's Wrath replaced by Ashen Resolve: brief damage resist [NEW] |
| Imperial | Imperial Luck (more gold found) | Voice of the Emperor: calm nearby people for 60 seconds |
| Khajiit | Claws (15 unarmed damage) | Night Eye: night vision for 60 seconds |
| Nord | Resist Frost 50 percent | Battle Cry: nearby enemies flee for 30 seconds |
| Orc | none | Berserker Rage: half damage taken, double damage dealt, 60 seconds |
| Redguard | Resist Poison 50 percent | Adrenaline Rush: Stamina regenerates much faster for 60 seconds |

**Interaction with Rage:** racial powers are separate from the Rage meter and Fury powers. Orc Berserker Rage and Nord Battle Cry overlap with Fury, so they share a short global cooldown so the two systems cannot be chained for free.

## Appearance and the family
The family matches the player's chosen race. Default names (Nadira, Tavi, Dune) are Redguard defaults. Other races get a name list per race.
